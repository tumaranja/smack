# Smack: Privacy policy

**Effective date: August 17, 2026**

Smack is a browser extension that lets you hide posts from your LinkedIn
and X feeds with a playful animation. This policy describes what data the
extension handles. The short version: **Smack collects no data, and
nothing you do ever leaves your browser.**

## Data collection

Smack does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any data. It has no
analytics, no telemetry, no accounts, and no servers of its own. It makes
no network requests.

## What is stored, and where

Your settings are saved with Chrome's built-in extension storage
(`chrome.storage.sync`): your list of smack styles (up to five
emoji-and-word pairs, in the order you arrange them) and an on/off
sound preference. This keeps them on your device
and, if you're signed into Chrome with sync enabled, syncs them across
your own browsers via your Google account, like any Chrome setting. The
developer has no access to them.

Hiding a post uses the site's own feature, exactly as if you had used it
yourself: LinkedIn's "hide post" button, or X's "Not interested in this
post" menu item. Posts the site itself offers no
hide for, such as your own, get no smack button at all.

## Permissions

- **`storage`**: saves the settings described above.
- **Access to `linkedin.com` and `x.com` pages**: the extension runs
  only on those two sites, where it adds its button to feed posts. It
  does not read, record, or transmit page content.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this
